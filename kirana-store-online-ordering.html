How Kirana Stores Can Take Online Orders Without Paying High Commission

By Galibrand Team | Reading Time: 6 mins

The Indian retail market is undergoing a massive shift. Just a few years ago, customers would walk into their local Kirana store, chat with the owner, and buy their weekly groceries. Today, the scenario is changing rapidly. Customers, especially the younger generation, prefer the convenience of scrolling through an app and getting items delivered to their doorstep.

For a local shop owner, this presents a difficult choice: join the big aggregator apps and lose a huge chunk of profit, or stay offline and lose customers. But is there a third option? Can you have your own kirana store online order system without breaking the bank?

In this guide, we will explore why relying on third-party apps is hurting your business and how you can set up a zero commission online ordering system for your shop.

The Problem with Big Delivery Apps

It feels easy to sign up for popular food and grocery delivery platforms. They promise visibility and new customers. However, the math often doesn't work in favor of the small retailer.

1. High Commissions Eat Your Margins

Most aggregator platforms charge anywhere between 20% to 30% commission on every order. In the grocery business, margins are already thin—often hovering around 10-15%. If you pay 25% to an app, you are essentially paying out of your own pocket to sell your goods. You might be doing more sales, but you are making less profit.

2. Loss of Customer Connection

When a customer orders through a third-party app, they are the app's customer, not yours. You don't get their data, you can't build a relationship with them, and you can't offer them personalized "Udhaar" or loyalty benefits. If the app decides to delist you or increase commissions tomorrow, your business suffers immediately.

The Reality Check: If you sell ₹1,00,000 worth of groceries on a commission-based app, you might end up paying ₹25,000 just in fees. That is money that should have stayed in your family.

Is WhatsApp the Answer?

Many shop owners try to bypass commissions by taking orders on WhatsApp. While this is a great whatsapp order alternative to big apps, it comes with its own set of headaches as you scale.

  • The Chaos of Chat: "Bhaiya, 1kg aaloo bhej do." "Kaunsa wala? Pahadi ya Desi?" "Price kya hai?" This back-and-forth conversation for every single item consumes hours of your time.
  • Calculation Errors: Manually totaling bills from a chat list often leads to mistakes.
  • No Inventory Sync: A customer might order something that went out of stock an hour ago, leading to disappointment and cancellations.

WhatsApp is a communication tool, not a grocery store website India needs for efficient business management.

The Solution: Your Own Online Ordering System

The most sustainable way to grow your business is to have your own local shop online ordering system. This means a website or a web-app that carries your brand name, your logo, and your rules.

Here is why having your own platform wins:

  • Zero Commissions: You keep 100% of the profit. Whether you do 10 orders or 100 orders, your cost remains the same.
  • Brand Identity: Customers visit your link (e.g., sharmakiranastore.com), reinforcing your brand in their minds.
  • Direct Payments: Money goes directly to your UPI or bank account. No waiting for weekly settlements from aggregators.
  • Control Over Delivery: You decide the delivery radius. You know your local area better than any GPS map.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Do I need technical knowledge to run an online store?

No. Modern systems like Galibrand are designed to be as easy as using WhatsApp or Facebook. If you can send a message on a smartphone, you can manage your online store.

2. How do I deliver the orders?

You manage the delivery using your existing staff or delivery boys. Since you are targeting your local neighborhood (1-3 km radius), this is usually very cost-effective compared to hiring third-party logistics.

3. Is it expensive to build a grocery website?

Custom development can cost lakhs. However, subscription-based platforms (SaaS) allow you to start for as low as ₹999 per month, which is affordable for even small shops.

4. Can I accept online payments?

Yes. You can use your personal or business UPI QR codes (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) to accept payments directly. You can also offer Cash on Delivery (COD).

5. How do customers find my website?

You share your shop link via WhatsApp groups, status updates, and even print it on your bills or carry bags. Since you are targeting local loyal customers, direct sharing works best.
