🏁 Setup Guide

1. Quick Start: Launch Your Store in 15 Minutes

Welcome to Galibrand! Our platform is built specifically for Indian local businesses — including Kirana stores, vegetable and fruit shops, bakeries, and street food vendors. Follow these simple steps to go live today:

1

Sign Up & Claim Your Store Link

Our team sets up your unique link (e.g., galibrand.cloud/shop/your-dukaan) or your custom domain. You'll receive your login credentials via WhatsApp and SMS.

2

Enter Store Timings & Contact Info

Go to Settings > Shop Profile. Set your shop opening and closing hours, customer support phone number, and local address.

3

Set Your Delivery Area

Specify the local pincodes, colonies, or maximum delivery radius (e.g., within 3 km or 5 km) that your delivery boys cover.

4

Share Your Store Link on WhatsApp

Send your store link to your regular customers via WhatsApp. They can immediately open it in their browser and start ordering with zero app install required.

💡

Pro Tip: We provide free catalog upload assistance on all plans! Simply send us your price list or menu on WhatsApp, and our team will upload your items for you.

📦 Catalog & Inventory

2. Managing Products & Categories

Keep your digital shelves neat and attractive so your neighborhood customers can find what they need in seconds.

Adding Single Items

Enter product title, select category (e.g., Staples, Dairy, Oils, Spices), set retail price (MRP vs Selling Price), and upload a photo.

Instant Stock Toggle

Ran out of an item? Simply flip the toggle next to the product to mark it "Out of Stock" instantly so customers won't order it.

ℹ️

Image Quality: Clean square photos (1:1 aspect ratio, JPEG or WebP) taken on your smartphone work best. Crisp packaging photos increase orders by up to 40%!

⚖️ Vegetable & Fruit Shops

3. Daily Price Updates & Weight-Based Selling

Mandi rates change every single morning. Galibrand has built-in support for dynamic pricing and fractional weights tailored for vegetable vendors and wholesalers.

A

Morning Quick Price Update

Open the "Daily Rates" screen on your mobile dashboard. You can update the prices of all fresh veggies in under 60 seconds each morning before opening.

B

Weight Variations (250g, 500g, 1kg)

Customers can select exact quantities such as 250 grams of ginger, 500 grams of tomatoes, or 5 kg sacks of potatoes. The total price calculates automatically.

C

Wholesale & MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)

Wholesalers can set minimum batch orders (e.g., minimum 10 packs or 50kg) and show tier-discounted wholesale rates to retail clients.

💳 0% Commission Payments

5. Direct UPI QR Code & Cash on Delivery

Unlike third-party aggregator apps that hold your money for weeks and deduct 25-30% in commission, Galibrand takes 0% commission. You keep 100% of your earnings.

1

Your Own UPI QR Code

Upload your shop's PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm QR code in Settings > Payments. Customers pay directly into your bank account.

2

Cash on Delivery (COD)

Enable COD so customers can pay your delivery staff directly in cash or upon delivery.

3

Digital Khata / Udhaar Logs

Keep a digital ledger for trusted neighborhood families who settle their grocery bill on a weekly or monthly basis.

📍 Neighborhood Delivery

6. Delivery Areas, Pincodes & Free Delivery Limits

Configure exactly where you deliver and avoid orders from far away places you cannot service.

  • ✓
    Pincode Restrictions: Add the specific 6-digit postal pincodes your shop covers.
  • ✓
    Delivery Charges: Set a flat delivery fee (e.g. ₹20 or ₹30) for small orders.
  • ✓
    Free Delivery Threshold: Offer Free Delivery on orders above a specified amount (e.g. "Free Home Delivery on orders above ₹499").
  • ✓
    Store Pickup / Takeaway: Allow nearby customers to place their order online and pick up packed bags without waiting in line.
📱 Customer Experience

7. Customer PWA & WhatsApp Sharing

Your shop functions as a high-speed Progressive Web App (PWA). Customers do not need to download heavy apps from the Play Store or register complicated passwords.

Copy and customize this ready-to-send WhatsApp broadcast message for your regular customers:

Namaste! Hamari dukaan ab online ho gayi hai! 🛒 Ab aap ghar baithe taaze fal, sabzi aur kirana ka samaan mangwa sakte hain: https://galibrand.cloud/shop/apnidukaan Free Home Delivery uplabdh hai!
📲

Add to Home Screen: When customers visit your website on Chrome or Safari, they will see an "Add to Home Screen" prompt. Once tapped, your shop's app icon sits right on their phone screen like a native app!

🌐 Custom Brand

8. Connecting Your Custom Domain (.com / .in)

Strengthen your shop's independent brand with your very own domain name (such as www.rameshkirana.com).

DNS Configuration

Add these records in your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger):

CNAME  www  galibrand.cloud

Our technical support team can assist you or handle the entire DNS setup free of charge.
