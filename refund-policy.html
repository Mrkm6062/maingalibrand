Refund & Cancellation Policy

Transparent policies for your peace of mind.

Thank you for choosing Galibrand. We strive to provide the best online ordering system for your business. Please read our refund and cancellation policy carefully.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time. To cancel your subscription, please contact our support team at galibrand99@gmail.com or use the cancellation option in your dashboard (if available).

Effect of Cancellation: Upon cancellation, your service will continue until the end of your current billing period. After the billing period ends, your account will be downgraded or deactivated, and you will not be charged again.

2. Refund Policy

Galibrand operates on a prepaid subscription model (Monthly or Yearly).

  • No Refunds for Partial Months: We do not offer refunds or credits for partial months of service, upgrade/downgrade refunds, or refunds for months unused with an open account.
  • 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee: For new customers on the "Business" or "Enterprise" annual plans, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with our service within the first 7 days of your initial payment, you may request a full refund. This does not apply to monthly plans.

3. Technical Issues

If you are facing technical issues that prevent you from using our service, please contact our support team immediately. If we are unable to resolve a critical failure of the platform within a reasonable timeframe (72 hours), you may be eligible for a pro-rated refund for the downtime period.

4. Double Charges

In the event of a billing error or double charge, please contact us immediately with transaction details. We will investigate and issue a full refund for the erroneous charge within 5-7 business days.
