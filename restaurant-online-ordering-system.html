Industry Challenges

The Everyday Challenges Faced by Restaurants & Food Businesses

Operating a food business across cities in Uttar Pradesh—from Lucknow, Varanasi, and Kanpur to Jaunpur, Prayagraj, and Agra—means dealing with chaotic peak hours, rising costs, and aggressive third-party aggregators.

💸

Crushing 20% to 30% Commissions

Third-party food delivery apps take a massive cut from every order. On everyday meals, snacks, and sweets, this commission often wipes out the restaurant's entire net profit margin.

📞

Phone & WhatsApp Order Chaos

Taking orders through endless phone calls and scattered WhatsApp chats during morning breakfast and evening dinner rushes leads to misheard items, missing customer addresses, and delayed preparation.

📋

Difficulty Maintaining Digital Menus

Printed menus get dirty, tear easily, and become outdated whenever prices fluctuate or dishes go out of stock. Updating static PDF files shared over social media is cumbersome and unreadable on phones.

🔍

Limited Online Brand Visibility

When local customers search for food online, independent food businesses without an SEO-friendly website remain invisible, losing valuable neighborhood traffic to big franchised chains.

🔒

Zero Customer Data Ownership

Delivery aggregators mask customer phone numbers. You cannot reach out to your own regular patrons for festival specials, new dish launches, or loyalty offers, keeping you permanently dependent on third parties.

🧾

Disconnected Counter & Kitchen Flow

Hand-written paper kitchen slips cause confusion between billing staff and cooks. Orders get mixed up, dine-in tables wait too long, and managing parcel pickups alongside counter sales becomes stressful.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, authentic answers to common questions asked by restaurant and cloud kitchen owners looking to take their business online.

1. How can I create an online ordering website for my restaurant? ↓
You can create an online ordering website for your restaurant in minutes by registering on the Galibrand merchant portal at dashboard.galibrand.cloud. Enter your restaurant name to generate your dedicated storefront URL, choose a restaurant theme, add your food categories and dishes with photos and prices, configure your direct UPI payment QR or Cash on Delivery, and publish your website instantly without writing any code.
2. Can I create a website for my cloud kitchen? ↓
Yes. Galibrand provides a dedicated cloud kitchen online ordering system. You can configure your local delivery radius in kilometers, define delivery pincodes, set minimum order values, and route incoming orders to a Kitchen Display System (KDS) or WhatsApp without paying steep 20% to 30% aggregator commissions.
3. Can customers order food through WhatsApp? ↓
Yes. Galibrand includes automated WhatsApp ordering for restaurants. Customers can browse your digital menu, select items and quantities, and tap the WhatsApp order button to send a formatted order summary directly to your kitchen or counter WhatsApp number, including customer name, phone, item list, and delivery address.
4. Can I add food images and prices? ↓
Yes. Through the Galibrand dashboard, you can upload high-resolution food images, set regular selling prices and compare-at MRP discount prices, tag items as Veg or Non-Veg, and configure portion sizes or weight variants such as Half, Full, 250g, 500g, or 1kg.
5. Can I manage restaurant orders online? ↓
Yes. Galibrand provides a unified order management dashboard with real-time sound chimes for incoming orders. You can track orders from Pending to Preparing, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled, view live kitchen timers, and coordinate with delivery riders or takeaway counter staff.
6. Can I create a mobile-friendly restaurant website? ↓
Yes. Every Galibrand storefront is engineered as a lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA). It loads in under 2 seconds on all mobile browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox), requires no app store downloads or APK installations, and offers a smooth app-like ordering experience for smartphone users.
7. Can I use my own domain name? ↓
Yes. While every store receives a free custom sub-domain (such as yourrestaurant.galibrand.cloud), merchants on the Premium plan can easily connect their own custom top-level domain (such as www.yourrestaurantname.com) with complimentary SSL encryption.
8. Does Galibrand support cafes and takeaway businesses? ↓
Yes. Galibrand is tailored for cafes, tea bars, bakeries, fast-food outlets, and takeaway counters. It supports quick 3-click counter POS billing, takeaway token generation, contactless QR table menus for dine-in patrons, and rapid parcel checkout.
9. Can I configure delivery and pickup? ↓
Yes. You have full control over your fulfillment options. You can offer home delivery with customizable delivery radius perimeters, tiered delivery fees, and minimum order thresholds, as well as a self-pickup / takeaway option where customers place orders in advance and pick up their packed parcels.
10. How do I start using Galibrand? ↓
Getting started is simple. Visit dashboard.galibrand.cloud to create your free merchant account, enter your restaurant details, upload your menu items, and start accepting online and WhatsApp orders immediately. You can also explore our subscription options on our pricing page.
