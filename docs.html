Enter OTP & Verify Email

🍽️ Restaurant & Dine-In

6. Dining Tables & Table QR Menus

For cafes, cloud kitchens, and restaurants offering Dine-in contactless digital ordering.

Configuring Tables

  1. Go to Overview > Restaurant & Cafe Dining Setup tab.
  2. Toggle "Restaurant & Cafe Dining Setup" to ON.
  3. In Table Numbers, list your tables separated by commas (e.g. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, Outdoor-1, VIP-1).
  4. Click "Save Tables".

Table QR Codes for Contactless Ordering

GaliBrand generates unique QR codes for each table. Click "Print Table QRs" or "Download QR".

When a guest scans Table 4's QR code, their digital menu opens with Table 4 pre-locked, routing placed orders straight to the kitchen display!

📦 Products & Catalog

7. Adding Products & Veg / Non-Veg Settings

Creating, managing, and categorizing your items with custom variants and food types.

A. Adding a Single Product

  1. From the sidebar, click on "Manage Products".
  2. Click the green "+ Add Product" button in the top right.
  3. Fill in the product details:
    • Product Name: Public display title (e.g., Paneer Butter Masala).
    • Category: Assign to a category (create categories in Manage Category).
    • Selling Price (₹): The final price charged to the customer.
    • Compare-at Price (₹): Original MRP to show discount badge (e.g. ₹350 ₹299).
    • Stock Quantity: Current inventory count (or leave unchecked for unlimited stock).
  4. Product Images: Click to upload up to 5 high-resolution photos.
  5. Variants: If applicable, add sizes (Small, Medium, Large) or weights (250g, 500g, 1kg) with individual prices.
  6. Click "Save Product" to publish it immediately to your live storefront.

B. Selecting Food Type (Veg 🟢 / Non-Veg 🔴)

Essential for food, groceries, and dining establishments to show dietary symbols:

  1. Inside the Add/Edit Product modal, locate the "Food Type (Veg / Non-Veg)" dropdown.
  2. Select either:
    • 🟢 Veg (Green dot symbol displayed on store, cart, and KDS)
    • 🔴 Non-Veg (Red dot symbol displayed)
  3. Save the product. The icon displays prominently next to the item name on all customer menus and kitchen tickets.

C. 1-Click Starter Catalog Import

Don't want to type 50 products manually? Use the starter catalog:

  1. On Manage Products, click "Import Default Products".
  2. Select your industry (Grocery, Cafe, Bakery, Street Food, Fashion).
  3. Select the products with sample prices and photos.
  4. Click "Import Selected" to populate your store instantly!

D. Bulk Stock CSV Export & Import

To update prices or stock for hundreds of items in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets:

  1. Click "Export Stock CSV" to download your current spreadsheet.
  2. Open CSV in Excel. Update AddStock or CurrentStock.
  3. Save the file.
  4. Click "Import Stock CSV" and upload your file. All stocks update in seconds!
📜 Store Policies & Legal

8. Create & Manage Store Policies

Comply with e-commerce regulations by establishing legal and customer assurance policies. Online payment gateways like Razorpay require active store policies before approving live accounts.

  1. From the sidebar navigation, click on "Manage Policies".
  2. To create a new policy, enter:
    • Policy Title: e.g., Return & Refund Policy, Privacy Policy, or Terms & Conditions.
    • Policy Content: Type or paste your detailed guidelines, eligibility criteria, and contact email.
  3. Click "Save Policy".
  4. The policy is automatically published with a clean public URL (e.g. /policy/refund-policy) and linked in your storefront footer.
🚚 Neighborhood Delivery

9. Delivery & Shipping Settings

Control where you deliver, delivery fees, and free shipping conditions.

  1. Click on "Delivery Settings" in the sidebar.
  2. Configure your shipping rules:
    • Delivery Mode:
      • Deliver Everywhere: Accept orders from all locations across the country.
      • By Pincode: Restrict deliveries only to specific 6-digit postal pincodes (e.g. 110001, 110002). Highly recommended for local shops!
      • By State / District: Limit deliveries to selected states.
    • Base Delivery Charge: Fixed delivery fee added to cart (e.g. ₹40). Enter 0 for free delivery on all orders.
    • Free Shipping Threshold: Minimum order value to qualify for Free Shipping (e.g. enter 499 so orders of ₹500 or more get ₹0 delivery fee).
  3. Click "Save Delivery Configuration".
💳 0% Commission Payments

10. Checkout & Online Payments (Razorpay & COD)

Accept instant UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Cards, Netbanking, and Cash on Delivery.

  1. From your sidebar, open "Checkout & Payments".
  2. Cash on Delivery (COD): Toggle ON to accept cash payments upon doorstep delivery.
  3. Online Payments (Razorpay):
    • Toggle "Accept Online Payments" to ON.
    • Enter your Razorpay API Key ID (e.g. rzp_live_XXXXXXXXXX).
    • Enter your Razorpay API Key Secret (encrypted securely in the database).
  4. Click "Save Payment Settings".
💡
Direct Settlement: Customer payments go directly from Razorpay into your bank account on T+1 settlement without any platform middlemen!
👥 Operations & Staff

11. Manage Staff & Role-Based Permissions

Empower your team with individual logins for Counter, Kitchen, Cashier, and Delivery personnel.

Available Staff Roles

Role Permitted Terminal Access Capabilities
Counter / Cashier Counter POS Only Take walk-in orders, apply coupons, send digital WhatsApp bills with attached PDF, print thermal receipts.
Kitchen / Chef Kitchen Display (KDS) Only View incoming tickets, mark Preparing, Wasted, or Finish.
Delivery Rider Delivery Monitor Only View only assigned orders, 1-tap Call/WhatsApp/Google Maps, Collect UPI Payment, mark Delivered.
Store Manager All Terminals Full access to switch seamlessly between Counter, Kitchen, and Delivery operations.

How to Add a Staff Member

  1. In admin dashboard, click on "Manage Staff".
  2. Click "+ Add Staff".
  3. Fill in: Name, 10-digit Phone, Role, and Login Password.
  4. The system generates a unique Staff ID (e.g. STF-103).
  5. Click "Save Staff". Hand credentials to your employee.

Staff Login Portal

Staff members do not need admin access. They simply open:

https://yourstore.galibrand.cloud/staff-login

Staff enter their Staff ID / Phone and Password. The portal automatically restricts them to their assigned role!

🧾 POS, KDS & Fulfillment

12. Counter POS, Kitchen Display & Delivery Monitor

High-speed billing terminal with automated digital bill dispatch and order lifecycle management.

A. Creating a Sale at the Counter

  1. Open Counter POS (accessible via Manage Counter or /staff-login).
  2. Tap product cards or use the search bar to add items to cart.
  3. Select Dine-in (pick table number) or Takeaway.
  4. Enter customer mobile number (for digital receipt delivery).
  5. Choose payment mode: Cash, UPI, or Card.
  6. Click "Send to Kitchen & Create Order".

B. Sending Digital Bill with Attached PDF to WhatsApp

As soon as the sale completes, the Digital Bill Screen appears:

  • 💬 Send on WhatsApp (Attach PDF):
    • On mobile devices: Directly attaches the official Receipt_XXXXXX.pdf file into WhatsApp chat along with itemized summary!
    • On desktop browsers: Automatically downloads the PDF and opens WhatsApp Web with the bill link.
  • 📥 Download PDF Bill: Instantly saves the 80mm thermal PDF receipt to your device.
  • 🖨️ Print Receipt: Sends raw format to any connected thermal Bluetooth or USB receipt printer.

C. Kitchen Display System (KDS)

Kitchen staff view active orders in real time with audio chime alerts:

  • 🍳 Start Preparing: Changes status to preparing, showing kitchen elapsed timer.
  • ⚠️ Mark Wasted: If food is spilled or burnt, records as wasted with reason tracking for inventory accounting.
  • ✅ Finish:
    • If Dine-in: Transitions directly to delivered!
    • If Delivery: Modal opens prompting to assign an active delivery rider, moving order to shipped!

D. Delivery Rider Terminal & UPI Collection

When delivery staff log in, they only see orders assigned to them for dispatch:

  • 📞 1-Tap Call: Calls customer phone directly.
  • 💬 1-Tap WhatsApp: Opens chat with customer.
  • 🗺️ Google Maps: Opens driving directions to customer address.
  • 📲 Collect UPI Payment: Customer scans dynamic live UPI QR code with GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm. Once verified, records as PAID via UPI in real-time!
  • ✅ Mark Delivered: Rider marks order completed upon handing over parcel.
